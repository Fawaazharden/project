The gap

Where paid leads go quiet inside a CRM that already works.

FUB is not the problem. Unworked queues are. Most teams recognize at least one of these on sight.

Warm, not transfer-ready

Live transfers convert. Everything else stalls.

You hit new leads hard. Live transfers turn into listings. Warm leads that do not transfer get a few Action Plan touches, then sit — until a competitor stays in the conversation.

Aged & imported databases

200+ untouched leads on a new agent's plate.

Imports, sphere dumps, and agent-owned lists sit idle because qualifying and re-engaging them is manual. Action Plans pause on reply. Nobody has hours to restart every conversation.

Open houses

Sign-in sheets that never become pipeline.

Sunday traffic becomes Monday cleanup. Without consistent, compliant follow-up toward a showing or agent handoff, the list goes cold inside a week.

Assistive AI ceiling

Smart Messages help agents write. They do not work the lead.

FUB AI drafts, summarizes, and suggests tasks. An agent still has to approve, send, call, and chase. That is useful — and it is not an ISA.

Routing

Your routing stays yours.

Follow Up Boss already supports advanced lead flow — ZIP, city, state, tags, groups, ponds, round-robin, first-to-claim. The Reddit question is not “does FUB have territory routing?” It does. The question is whether your AI handoff honors it.

Example

Territory 1 → Agents 1, 2, 3. Territory 2 → Agents 3, 4, 5. A Territory 1 lead that qualifies only ever hands to Agents 1–3 (or stays with the current owner if agent-owned).

What we configure

Owner respect, pond eligibility, group membership, and tag/ZIP rules you already run — so AI help never leaks leads to the wrong desk.

FAQ

The questions you'd ask on the call.

How is this different from FUB Smart Messages and Action Plans?

Smart Messages draft replies for an agent to approve. Action Plans are time-based sequences (email, tasks, tags, stages). Native multi-step automated SMS typically needs a third party. We build an autonomous conversation agent that calls/texts, qualifies, nurtures, and writes structured results back to FUB — then humans take over.

Will every AI text show inside Follow Up Boss?

Yes. We set up AI SMS as a native FUB-visible workflow. Every inbound and outbound AI text can be seen on the lead inside Follow Up Boss, alongside the qualification fields, notes, tags, stages, scores, summaries, and handoff status.

Do we have to change our FUB phone number?

Usually not. We configure messaging around your existing setup and keep every AI text visible in FUB. If your specific number or carrier setup requires porting, we map that before build instead of surprising you after launch.

Will Follow Up Boss ban my account?

No. We use FUB’s documented open API with proper registration headers and your API key. No scraping, no auth bypass. FUB is designed for integrations across their 250+ partner ecosystem.

Does this respect ponds, groups, and territory routing?

Yes. FUB already routes by ZIP, city, tags, groups, and ponds. During setup we map your rules and make sure AI handoffs only go to eligible owners/agents — including “Territory 1 only to Agents 1–3” style logic.

Can we re-engage imported / aged databases?

Yes — with consent and compliance rules. We do not cold-blast lists that fail TCPA / carrier / your brokerage policy. Setup includes suppressions, opt-outs, and who is eligible for AI outreach.

Why not just switch to GHL / Lofty / kvCORE?

Those platforms can be right if you need bundled IDX, lead gen, or a full rebuild. If you like FUB and only need autonomous follow-up, switching recreates ponds, routing, reporting, and agent habits for a problem we can solve as an add-on. Many teams reconsider once they price the migration risk.

What about Zillow and FUB privacy?

Zillow owns Follow Up Boss and updated privacy terms around Zillow Pro / mutual customers. Zillow states agent-only contacts are not sold or shared with other agents. We are not affiliated with Zillow. If privacy posture is your reason to leave FUB, evaluate their current notice yourself — we will not fear-sell that angle.

Why is this so cheap vs $1,000/mo tools?

We charge for build and support, not a recurring product SKU. You pay raw telephony and model costs on your accounts. Managed AI ISAs fund product, sales, compliance ops, and margin every month. Different business model — pick which fits.
