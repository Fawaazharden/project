Control model

Automation without losing operator control.

The production system is designed so your team can activate, pause, inspect, and hand off at both the global and contact level.

01

Contact-level activation

An explicit tag or eligibility state determines whether AI may engage a specific record.

02

Global kill switch

Pause new automated activity while preserving existing FUB data and conversation history.

03

Visible audit trail

Operators can see what happened, what the AI learned, and why a transfer or assignment occurred.
