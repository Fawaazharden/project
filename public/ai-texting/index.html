Conversation paths

More than a scheduled drip.

The AI responds to what the lead says, captures qualification, and knows when to continue, suppress, or hand over.

01

New lead response

Start an approved conversation based on source, stage, tag, ownership, and timing rules.

02

Missed-call recovery

Continue a voice attempt by SMS without asking the lead to repeat the same context.

03

Property-link follow-up

Share useful listing searches, then respond differently when intent becomes stronger.

04

Long-term nurture

Work approved warm leads over time with frequency limits, stop conditions, and human takeover.

05

Structured write-back

Record conversation history, summaries, fields, tags, stages, tasks, and handoff status.

06

Suppression controls

Respect opt-outs, quiet hours, excluded sources, owners, active conversations, and global pause rules.

Agent experience

The AI works behind FUB, not beside it.

Agents should not need another inbox to understand the lead. They receive the conversation, qualification, and recommended next action inside the CRM they already open.

  • Inbound and outbound message records associated with the correct contact.
  • Clear AI-active, nurture, qualified, handed-off, and suppressed states.
  • Human reply or operator action can pause automation.
  • Property context and lead preferences remain attached to the record.
