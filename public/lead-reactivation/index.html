A controlled reactivation process

Do not blast the entire database.

Start with eligibility, relevance, and a measured cohort. The goal is a credible conversation rather than activity for its own sake.

01

Segment

Filter by source, stage, age, owner, geography, consent, previous response, property activity, and exclusion state.

02

Re-engage

Use a useful reason to reconnect: updated search, property link, changed need, market question, or unfinished inquiry.

03

Qualify and route

Capture current intent, suppress disinterest, nurture timing, and hand ready opportunities to the right agent.

Intent signals

Use what FUB and your website can reliably tell you.

Property views, repeat visits, saved searches, lead replies, source, stage history, and previous conversation can improve relevance, but only when those events are actually captured.

  • Audit activity coverage before building an intent score.
  • Treat repeated activity across different days as stronger than rapid repeat clicks in one session.
  • Use property links to recover useful behavior and improve future follow-up.
  • Keep inferred intent clearly separate from verified lead statements.
