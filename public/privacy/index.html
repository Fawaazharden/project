Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VOCALXLABS LLC ("Vocalxlabs," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit vocalxlabs.com, contact us, or use our services. By using our website or services, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically.

  • Information you provide: your name, business name, email address, phone number, market or service area, and any details you share when you book a call, submit a form, or message us.
  • Automatically collected: IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring pages, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Booking data: when you schedule a call, our scheduling provider (Calendly) collects the information you enter to book.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule and conduct calls.
  • To provide, operate, and improve our services.
  • To send service-related and, where permitted, marketing communications.
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS and Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in, you may receive text messages (SMS) from Vocalxlabs relating to your inquiry, appointment reminders, account and service updates, and, where you have consented, offers.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use of text messaging originator opt-in data and consent is excluded from sharing, and this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how the site works.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, hosting, scheduling, analytics, and messaging vendors), bound by confidentiality obligations.

As stated above, text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

8. Your Rights and Choices
