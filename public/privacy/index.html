Privacy

How we handle information submitted through this site.

This page summarizes the privacy posture for the dedicated Follow Up Boss implementation site.

Last updated: August 7, 2026. The primary VocalX Labs privacy policy is also available at vocalxlabs.com/privacy.

Information we collect

We may receive information you voluntarily provide through email, telephone, scheduling, or project conversations, such as your name, company, contact details, CRM workflow, lead sources, and implementation requirements.

Scheduling and embedded services

The site embeds Calendly for scheduling and may load media or infrastructure from third-party providers. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

How information is used

  • Respond to requests and schedule conversations.
  • Evaluate technical and operational fit.
  • Prepare proposals, implementation plans, and support.
  • Protect the site and comply with legal obligations.

Project data

Client credentials, lead data, and system information are handled only for the agreed implementation and support purpose. Public case studies should not expose passwords, API keys, private contact details, or identifying client data beyond the approved scope.
