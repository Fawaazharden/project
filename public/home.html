FAQ

The questions we get on every call.

Will my agents have to learn another tool?

No. Conversations, summaries, fields, notes and assignment all land on the Follow Up Boss person record they already open. Turning the AI off for one contact is a tag they apply in the CRM.

What does it cost to run each month?

You pay your carrier for messages and minutes, your model provider for the AI, and a small hosting bill. All of it direct, none of it to us. Volume decides the number, and we size it with you on the call.

Do we have to change our phone number?

Usually not. Messaging and voice get mapped around your current setup, and anything that needs porting comes up in discovery rather than after the build. Carrier registration for the sending number we handle with you.

What if it says something wrong to a lead?

Remove the tag on that contact and it stops mid conversation, and a human takes over. There is a global stop for everything already in flight. Every message and call is on the record with a timestamp, so you can read exactly what was said.

How does it know what a lead looked at?

Your site already logs it and Follow Up Boss already stores it: which listings a person opened, when, and how many separate days they came back. We read that alongside the criteria they gave us in conversation, and match new listings against both.

It is also what wakes an aged lead up. Somebody who went quiet in March and started opening listings again last night gets worked tonight, where consent, brokerage policy, carrier rules and opt outs permit. Eligible segments get defined and approved before anything sends.

What happens if we stop working together?

Nothing switches off. The accounts, the number, the keys and the data are all in your name, so the system keeps running and you keep paying your vendors directly.
