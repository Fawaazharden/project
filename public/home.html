What actually happens on a team

Your agents are not the problem.
The way leads reach them is.

Every team we open up has good people in it. What they do not have is anything that decides who gets called first. So the decision gets made by whoever is free, whoever sounded keen on a form, and whoever happens to scroll far enough down the list.

01

Lead distribution is a coin flip

The same lead goes to your best agent and your newest one. One works it for three weeks. The other lets it sit. You paid the same for both.

02

Your top producer is the ceiling

They close, so you feed them more. Everyone else gets less and produces less. The team quietly compounds around one person and stalls at whatever they can personally carry.

03

Agents cherry-pick, and they are right to

They call the ones who sounded ready and leave the rest. No agent has ever been wrong to do that. It just means most of your lead budget funds the few who wrote a good form note.

04

The gap between assigned and called

A lead lands, gets an owner, then sits two days because that agent was at an inspection. Nobody owns that gap and nothing reports on it. It is the most expensive silence in your business.

05

You find out on the 30th, not the 5th

By the time a quiet month shows up in the numbers, the leads that caused it are three weeks cold. You are always diagnosing something you can no longer fix.

06

So recruiting becomes the only lever

You add agents. The same leads split more ways, per-agent production drops, and you have grown headcount instead of closings. Then you do it again next year.

What half a point is worth

You do not need a bigger number at the top.

Take a team running 300 leads a month and closing 3 percent. That is nine closings. Move it to 3.5 percent and it is ten and a half. At a $9,000 average commission, half a percentage point is roughly $162,000 a year, from the same roster and the same lead spend.

We are not going to promise you a tripling. Nobody credible can. Half a point is the most defensible number in this industry and it is already worth more than another hire.

How we build it

Four layers. In this order, every time.

AI on top of a broken CRM does not fix the CRM. It makes the mistakes faster and more expensive. So we do not start with AI. We start with the foundation, because that is where everything else has to land.

01Foundation

A CRM the team can work from

Stages simplified and defined. Lead flows so every source arrives with the right owner and the right plan attached. Smart lists ranked by who to call first, not by what happens to exist. Ponds, routing rules, duplicates and dead records cleared out. Action plans and drips rewritten so they read like a person wrote them.

This is the whole of what a CRM setup shop delivers. For us it is layer one.

02Signal

Who is actually ready, and why

The Follow Up Boss Pixel installed and verified, not attempted. Then every lead's behaviour read continuously: which listings they open, how many times, how recently, what they search for, what they save, and the moment they come back after going quiet.

It arrives as smart lists and tags inside the CRM your agents already use. There is no second screen.

03Conversation

Every lead worked, every day

AI that works the list the signal layer produces. It texts and calls in your brand from your numbers, holds a real two-way conversation, qualifies on area, price, timeline and motivation, and references the exact listings that lead keeps going back to. It books the appointment, routes it to the right agent on your rules, and writes every field, note and message onto the person record.

A per-contact off switch and a global stop, always. You decide who it is allowed to talk to.

04Visibility

One page, every Monday

Speed to first touch, attempts made, conversations held, appointments set, appointments held. By agent. So when a month goes quiet you know which of the three possible reasons it was, on the 5th instead of the 30th.

It arrives in your inbox. There is nothing for you to log into and check.

The part nobody else installs

The lead who opened the same three-bed twice this week does not need another drip email.

They need a call in the next hour. Right now, nobody on your team knows they exist.

Every lead in your database is quietly telling you what they want. Which property they came back to. How many times. What they searched next. Whether they turned up again at 9:40 on a Tuesday night after three weeks of silence.

That behaviour is already being captured. On almost every team we open up, nothing is being done with it. The lead sits in the same drip as the four thousand people who are not ready, and the agent calls whoever is at the top of a list sorted by date.

We change what the list is sorted by. When someone starts behaving like a buyer they move to the top, the system reaches out the same hour, and the message names the actual property they have been looking at. Not a template. The three-bed on Maple they keep going back to.

To be exact about what we see: behaviour on your own search site, through the Follow Up Boss Pixel. Which listings they open, how often, how recently, and what they search for. If that is not set up, it is one of the first things we fix, and it is yours.

Questions

Before you book.

Do my agents have to learn anything?

No. Everything appears inside the Follow Up Boss they already have open. The conversations land on the person record, the ready leads land in the smart lists they already call from, and the appointments land on their calendar. The only thing that changes for an agent is what is at the top of their list.

How much disruption is this for the team?

Close to none. Your agents keep working exactly as they are while we build. There is no migration, no downtime and nothing for them to install. On the day it goes live their list is simply better ordered and some of their leads have already been spoken to.

Do you replace Follow Up Boss?

No, and we would talk you out of it. Follow Up Boss is the right CRM for this. Everything we build lives inside the account you already have. Nothing migrates and nothing gets exported.

Follow Up Boss is adding its own AI. Why do I need you?

Follow Up Boss will draft the message. It will not make the call. Their AI summarises a call you made, suggests a reply you send, and surfaces a task you click. Every one of those still needs a person to be there and press something. We build the part that runs when nobody is there.

We already had our Follow Up Boss set up by someone.

Good, that is a real head start. That is layer one of four. A well organised CRM still needs a human to work it every day, and it still cannot tell your agents which lead came back to the same listing three times last week.

We tried AI before and nothing changed.

Most of the AI sold into this industry writes listing descriptions and social posts. It saves time and it cannot change a conversion rate, because nothing about it touches a lead. We only build AI that talks to your database.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the account and how much is being built, which is why we price it after the audit rather than before. Three things we can tell you now: it is a one-time build, there is no charge when your leads reply, and there is no monthly platform fee, because there is no platform. If the audit shows the numbers do not work for your operation, we will tell you that too.

How long does it take?

The written report comes back within two days of the audit call. Most builds are running against real leads inside fifteen days. Larger accounts and heavier custom work take longer, and we will tell you which one you are on the audit call.

What do you need from me?

A kickoff session and a review call. That is close to all of it. We do the pixel install, the carrier registration, the compliance setup, the content and the build.

Is this compliant?

Carrier registration, opt-out handling and a full timestamped audit trail are part of the build. The accounts are in your name, which means your compliance posture is yours to see and yours to control. TCPA exposure in this industry is rising quickly and we would rather over-engineer this part.

What happens if we stop working with you?

Everything keeps running. The accounts, the numbers, the data and the documentation are already yours. That is the point of building it this way.

We are not on Follow Up Boss.

Most of what we build is Follow Up Boss native and that is deliberate. Tell us what you run and we will tell you honestly whether it is a fit.
