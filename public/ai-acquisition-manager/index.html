What is Vocalxlabs?

Vocalxlabs (VOCALXLABS LLC) is a done-for-you lead generation company for United States real estate wholesalers and investors. Vocalxlabs is not self-serve software and not a cold calling platform. We build, run, and manage AI cold SMS outbound on your behalf: the system texts property owners, holds real conversations, qualifies motivated sellers on motivation, asking price, condition and repairs, and timeline, then hands you the ones ready to talk. We also build AI follow-up systems inside CRMs you already use, including Follow Up Boss and ReSimpli. Vocalxlabs is based in Sheridan, Wyoming and every engagement starts with a free 2-week pilot.

What is the AI Acquisition Manager?

The AI Acquisition Manager is a done-for-you system that generates and qualifies motivated seller leads for real estate wholesalers and investors. It runs AI cold SMS to your market, holds real conversations, and qualifies every interested seller on motivation, asking price, condition and repairs, and timeline, then hands you the ones ready to talk. Vocalxlabs builds, runs, and manages it, so you just take the calls.

I've been burned before. Why is this different?

Because you don't pay setup until it works. We start with a free 2-week pilot on real sellers in your market. You see qualified sellers land in your pipeline before any setup fee is due. The risk sits with us, not you.

The tech is the easy part. The build, the scripts, the qualification logic, the daily management, and the deliverability are what actually produce sellers. We do all of it and run it for you, so it works instead of sitting there half-configured.
